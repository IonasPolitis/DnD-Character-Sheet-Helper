## Mutagen Options
---
The mutagens are presented here in alphabetical order. If a mutagen has a level written beside it, you must be an Order of the Mutant Blood Hunter of that level to choose that mutagen.

#### Aether (Level 11):
You gain a Flying Speed of 30 feet for 1 hour.

#### Alluring (Level 7):
You have advantage on Charisma checks. Additionally, you are under the effect of the Alter Self spell, taking only the Change Appearance option, for 1 hour.

#### Celerity:
Your Dexterity increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood Hunter level 15.

#### Conversant (Level 7):
You have advantage on Intelligence checks, and are under the effect of the Tongues spell.

#### Cruelty (Level 11):
When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make an attack as a Bonus Action.

#### Echolocation (Level 11):
You have Blindsight up to 30 feet, or your Blindsight is increased by 30 feet. This Blindsight doesn't function if you are Deafened.

#### Embers:
You have Resistance to Fire damage.

#### Gelid:
You have Resistance to Cold damage.

#### Impermeable:
You have Resistance to Piercing damage.

#### Mobile:
You have Immunity to being Grappled or Restrained. At Blood Hunter level 11, you also have Immunity to being Paralyzed.

#### Nighteye:
You have Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet, or your Darkvision is increased by 60 feet.

#### Percipient:
You have advantage on Wisdom checks.

#### Potency:
Your Strength increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood Hunter level 15.

#### Precision:
Your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. At Blood Hunter level 15, it instead scores a critical hit on a roll of 18, 19, or 20.

#### Rapidity:
Your speed increases by 10 feet. At Blood Hunter level 15, it increases by an additional 5 feet.

#### Reagent (Level 11):
When you consume this mutagen, you can choose a mutagen you have concocted. This mutagen gains the effect of that mutagen. You can't concoct this mutagen again until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Reconstruction (Level 7):
You begin to rapidly heal. At the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to half your Blood Hunter level for 1 minute.

#### Sagacity:
Your Intelligence increases by 3, and so does your maximum. This increases to 4 at Blood
Hunter level 15.

#### Shielded:
You have Resistance to Slashing damage.

#### Unbreakable:
You have Resistance to Bludgeoning damage.

#### Vermillion:
You gain an additional use of your Hemocraft.
